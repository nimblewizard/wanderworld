YOUR WORLD REVEALED

WanderWorld turns your everyday journeys into an adventure. Walk, run, bike or drive through your city and watch the fog lift — revealing every street, trail and hidden corner you've explored. As you move, the app automatically looks up Wikipedia articles about nearby landmarks, parks and places of interest, narrating them aloud so you learn while you explore. Share your fog-of-war map and session cards with friends, challenge them to beat your distance, and cache tiles to keep exploring even when you go offline.
